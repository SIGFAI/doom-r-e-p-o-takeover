# R.E.P.O. Takeover

**Doom** × R.E.P.O. · visual · idea by the team

Bring R.E.P.O. into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> R.E.P.O. Takeover: rubber ducks, knife gnomes and sack-faced hunters invade Doom, and you haul their loot to hit quota. Ducks smash vases.

## Install

Copy mod.pk3 next to GZDoom and run: gzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file mod.pk3. Kill monsters, walk near the gold to grab it, and reach the quota on the top-left counter.
